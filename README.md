# Customer Behavior Analysis
## Objective
The objective of this project is to analyze customer shopping behavior using SQL, Python, and Power BI to identify purchasing trends, understand customer preferences, and generate actionable business insights that can improve customer engagement, marketing strategies, and overall sales performance.
## Dataset used
<a href="https://github.com/Deepak-NEGII/Customer-trends-data-analysis/blob/main/customer_shopping_behavior.csv">Dataset</a>
## Questions
- What is the total revenue generated by male and female customers?
- Which customers used discounts and spent more than the average purchase amount?
- Which products received the highest average customer review ratings?
- How does customer spending differ between Standard and Express shipping methods?
- Do subscribed customers contribute more revenue than non-subscribed customers?
- Which products have the highest percentage of discounted purchases?
- How are customers distributed across New, Returning, and Loyal segments?
- What are the top-selling products within each product category?
- Are repeat buyers more likely to subscribe to the company's services?
- How does revenue contribution vary across different age groups?
## Process 
Collected and explored the retail customer shopping dataset.
Cleaned and transformed the data using Python by handling missing values, correcting data types, and preparing the dataset for analysis.
Imported the cleaned data into SQL and performed business-focused queries to analyze customer behavior, spending patterns, product performance, and customer loyalty.
Conducted exploratory data analysis (EDA) to identify trends, correlations, and key purchase drivers.
Created interactive Power BI dashboards to visualize sales performance, customer demographics, product ratings, discount usage, and revenue trends.
Interpreted the results and generated actionable business recommendations to improve customer engagement, marketing effectiveness, and overall sales performance.

## Project Insights
- Male customers generated significantly higher revenue (157,890) compared to female customers (75,191), indicating that males contributed a larger share of total sales.
- Several customers used discounts while still spending above the average purchase amount, suggesting that discounts can effectively encourage higher-value purchases.
- Gloves, Sandals, Boots, Hat, and Skirt received the highest average customer ratings, making them the most positively reviewed products.
- Customers choosing Express shipping spent slightly more on average (60.48) than those using Standard shipping (58.46), indicating a willingness to pay for faster delivery.
- Non-subscribers contributed substantially more total revenue (170,436) than subscribers (62,645) due to their larger customer base, while average spending was nearly identical across both groups.
- Hat, Sneakers, Coat, Sweater, and Pants showed the highest percentage of discounted purchases, suggesting that sales promotions strongly influence demand for these products.
- The majority of customers were classified as Loyal (3,116), followed by Returning (701), with only a small number of New customers (83), indicating strong customer retention.
- Top-performing products across categories included Jewelry, Blouse, Sandals, and Jacket, highlighting the most popular items in Accessories, Clothing, Footwear, and Outerwear categories.
- Most repeat buyers were not subscribed to the company's membership program, suggesting an opportunity to improve subscription adoption among highly engaged customers.
- Young Adults generated the highest revenue contribution (62,143), followed by Middle-aged customers (59,197), making these age groups the most valuable customer segments for marketing efforts.
## Conclusion
This project explored customer shopping behavior and identified key trends in revenue, customer segments, product performance, and purchasing preferences. The insights gained can help businesses improve marketing strategies, enhance customer engagement, and make data-driven decisions to drive sales growth.
